Straight answers

Questions people actually ask

My books are a mess and I am embarrassed about it

Books that are behind or out of balance are exactly what the cleanup service is for. I do not need your books to be good before I start. I need your QuickBooks file and your statements. Nobody is judging the shoebox.

Do you get access to my bank accounts?

No. You invite me as an accountant user inside QuickBooks Online. You never give me a password, I never touch your bank login, and you can remove my access in two clicks at any time.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Monthly bookkeeping is month to month. No annual contract and no setup fee. Cancel with 30 days notice.

Can you do my taxes?

No. I am a bookkeeper, not a CPA, so I do not prepare or file tax returns. What I do is hand your tax preparer clean, reconciled books so the filing is quick and cheap.

What if I already have a CPA?

That works well. Most CPAs would rather receive finished books than build them from receipts. I coordinate with yours directly at tax time.

What does it cost and how fast do you reply?

Monthly bookkeeping starts at $250 a month, cleanup is $75 an hour, and setup is $300 flat. I answer calls, texts and emails within one business day, usually the same day.
