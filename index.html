About Sam Johnson

I am an Intuit certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor holding four Intuit certifications: Intuit Bookkeeping, QuickBooks Online Level 1, QuickBooks Online Level 2, and Intuit Enterprise Suite. I run Johnson Innovation Group out of Sierra Madre, California.

I played football at USC and with the Raiders. That taught me something simple that applies directly to bookkeeping: consistency beats talent. Books do not get messy because the work is hard. They get messy because nobody does it the same way every month. I do.

I am a bookkeeper, not a CPA, so I do not prepare or file tax returns. What I do is hand your tax preparer a clean, reconciled set of books, and work with them directly so nothing gets lost between us.
